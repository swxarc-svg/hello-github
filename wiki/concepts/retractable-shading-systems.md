## Definition

Retractable shading systems are architectural elements that can transform between deployed (shading) and stowed (compact) states. In the context of typhoon-prone humid-hot cities, retractability serves a dual purpose: providing shade and ventilation during normal conditions while reducing wind load exposure during extreme weather events.
